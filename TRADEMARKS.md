# NCode Trademarks

The names and marks of NCode Group are not covered by this repository's Apache License 2.0. They are copyright NCode
Group, all rights reserved, and this page says how you may use them.

## What It Covers

- The names: **NCode**, **NCode Group** and **NCode Identity**, and the name of any other NCode product.
- The marks: the `{N}` mark and the `{id}` mark, alone, with a wordmark, on a tile or in any other form, and every
  file in [logo/](logo/README.md).

The Apache License 2.0 covers this repository's code, tokens, stylesheets and themes. Its section 6 grants no
permission to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks or product names, beyond describing where a
work comes from. The fonts in [fonts/](fonts/) keep their own license, the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## What You May Do Without Asking

Use the unmodified names and marks to refer to an NCode project, for example:

- in an article, a talk, a video or a book about it;
- in a package listing, a catalog or a comparison;
- in a "works with" or "built with" statement about your own project;
- in a link to it, or in documentation of your own that uses it.

Take the files from a release of this repository as they are, and follow [the guide's Marks section](guidelines/01-marks.md)
for clear space, size and color.

## What You May Not Do

- Alter a mark: recolor it outside its palette, stretch, outline or redraw it, set its braces in a font, or combine it
  with another mark.
- Use a name or a mark as the name or logo of your own product, company, service or domain, or as part of one.
- Use a name or a mark in a way that suggests NCode Group makes, sponsors or endorses your product.

## Modified Distributions

A fork or a modified distribution of an NCode project replaces the names and the marks with its own. The code's
license lets you change and share the code; it does not let a changed version carry NCode's names or marks. Keep the
copyright and license notices the code's license asks for.

## Earlier Copies

Copies of the logo kit published in NCodeGroup/project-template, in its `brand/` folder from v0.93.0 on, were released
under that repository's MIT License. Those copies stay under the license they were published with. The marks in this
repository are released under the terms on this page.

## Ask

For any other use, open an issue in [NCodeGroup/brand](https://github.com/NCodeGroup/brand/issues) that says what you
want to do. Report a security problem privately instead, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.
